5. The math in your own words (say each unaided)

Circle method. One player sits fixed, the rest sit on a ring, you pair across the ring and rotate one seat per round. Opposite seats share a constant position sum and each rotation shifts that sum by minus 2. Since n is even, n minus 1 is odd, so 2 is invertible mod n minus 1, and the map r to minus 2r sweeps every residue class exactly once. Each pair has one fixed sum, so each pair meets exactly once.

Byes. A schedule is an edge coloring of K n. The chromatic index is n minus 1 for even n and n for odd n, so an odd field must rest one player per round. The app adds a phantom seat and everyone rests exactly once.

Elo and the race. Expectation is 1 over 1 plus 10 to the rating gap over 400; the update K times S minus E with K equal 32 is zero sum. Matches race to 5 racks, and a long race amplifies a small edge, so using E as the rack probability would over reward favorites. I invert the negative binomial by bisection to find the rack probability whose match odds equal E.

Seeding. The doubling recurrence puts seed s with seed 2k plus 1 minus s. The top 2 to the t seeds land in 2 to the t different subtrees (subtree isolation lemma), and two players meet at the root of the smallest subtree containing both, so 1 and 2 meet only in the final.

Double elimination. Two losses and you are out. The k minus 1 non champions carry two losses each, the champion carries zero or one, so 2k minus 2 matches, or 2k minus 1 with a grand final reset. The losers bracket alternates minor and major rounds and crosses the drops to the opposite side of the draw to delay rematches.

Carry-over. G counts opponent handoffs between consecutive rounds; imbalance is the sum of squares. Balanced schedules reach n times n minus 1; the circle method provably reaches the maximum (Lambrechts, Ficker, Goossens, Spieksma 2018), and balanced ones exist when n is a power of two (Russell 1980). I state the weakness and draw the matrix live.

7. Question drill (most likely oral questions)

Why does the proof need 2 to be invertible?
Rounds select pair sums minus 2r. If 2 were not a unit, that map would repeat some residue classes and skip others, so some pairs would meet twice and others never. n even makes n minus 1 odd, which guarantees it.
Why not use the Elo expectation directly as the rack probability?
A race to 5 amplifies a per rack edge. The 70 percent favorite would win the match more than 85 percent of the time. Inverting the formula keeps match odds exactly at 70.
Why can seeds 1 and 2 only meet in the final?
Subtree isolation: every subtree with 2 to the j leaves holds exactly one top seed of the matching tier, and two players meet at their subtrees' lowest common ancestor, which for seeds 1 and 2 is the root.
Why is double elimination exactly 2k minus 2 matches?
Count losses instead of matches. Every match produces one loss, every non champion exits with exactly two, the champion has zero or one.
Can rematches happen in the losers bracket?
Yes, once it narrows to single matches; the crossing of drops only delays them. At k equals 4 the losers final is forced, so a rematch is already possible there.
If the circle method is worst for carry-over, why use it?
It is the simplest construction with a short complete proof, which is the point in a math course. Balanced schedules exist for powers of two but lose the one line argument. I state the tradeoff and draw the matrix live rather than hide it.
Is your schedule the only valid one?
No. K8 alone has 6240 one-factorizations. The circle method is one systematic route with a provable construction.
